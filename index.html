Methodology Note

Structural Breaks Harmonization & Data Sanitization Engine

* Project Lead & Pipeline Developer: Ketrapal Konar | Series Code: RBI/SIBC/10_SECTORS

Longitudinal Time-Series Concordance & Stationarity Guarantee

This research platform applies continuous backward geometric chain-linking ratio splicing across Reserve Bank of India Sectoral Deployment of Bank Credit returns (Form VII / Table 4). The routine neutralizes artificial level jumps induced by central bank classification overhauls in 2007, 2017, and 2020.

1. Base-Year Rebasings & Splice Ratios

2007 Reclassification RBI Master Circular
Sectoral Template Restructuring

RBI consolidated Form VII reporting categories and aligned Scheduled Commercial Bank disclosures with revised industrial definitions.

k₂₀₀₇ = Y(Mar 2007)ⁿᵉʷ / Y(Mar 2007)ᵒˡᵈ

Backward levels are scaled by 12-month overlapping geometric splice factors to preserve pre-2007 growth trajectories.

2017 PSL Shift Revised PSL Norms
Priority Sector Lending Realignment

Full implementation of the 2015–16 revised PSL guidelines took effect in FY2017, segregating Small & Marginal Farmers and re-weighting export credit.

k₂₀₁₇ = Σ PSL_Adj / Base_Weight

Spliced chain adjustment harmonizes Agriculture and allied components across pre- and post-2017 priority classifications.

2020 MSME Criteria Gazette Jul 1, 2020
Composite Investment & Turnover

Government of India and RBI replaced plant-and-machinery limits with composite thresholds (Investment + Turnover up to ₹250 Cr for Medium enterprises).

k₂₀₂₀ = Y(Jul 2020)ⁿᵉʷ / Y(Jun 2020)ᵃᵈʲ

Reclassification rebalanced approximately 14.8% of enterprise loans into MSMEs; series spliced to prevent spurious structural spikes.

2. Strict Client-Side Data Sanitizer Engine

Incoming observations and published tables frequently contain regulatory footnote tokens that break econometric estimators (e.g. Stata tsset or R as.Date()). The embedded sanitizer operates recursively across all records:

Provisional / Revised Flags P, e, r, (P), (R) Regex Strip: /[Per]/gi → "" Uncorrupted IEEE 754 float
Missing / Withheld Values -, .., N.A., NA, nil Token Conversion → null Proper missing-data representation (no zero biasing)
